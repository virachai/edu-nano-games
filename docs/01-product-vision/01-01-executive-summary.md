## Executive Summary: nano-games

### 1. Vision Statement

**nano-games** is a collection of small, web-based educational mini-games that make learning fun and accessible for students from **elementary school through high school**. Every game runs directly in the browser — no installation required — so teachers and students can play instantly on a computer, tablet, or smartphone.

### 2. Problem Statement

- **Classroom engagement gap:** Traditional worksheets and lectures struggle to hold students' attention, especially for subjects like vocabulary, arithmetic, and chemistry.
- **Friction in getting started:** Many educational tools require accounts, installs, or paid licenses, creating barriers for schools with limited IT resources.

### 3. Solution & Value Proposition

- **Instant, web-based play:** Pure HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) games that open directly in any modern browser.
- **Curriculum-aligned content:** Games mapped to grade bands — from early elementary vocabulary to high school chemistry.
- **Three flagship titles:** Vocabulary Matching (early–upper elementary), Math Guardian Spaceship (upper elementary–middle school), and Chemistry Quiz Adventure (middle school–high school).
- **Built with Freebuff:** The project is scaffolded and co-developed with **Freebuff**, an AI-powered coding agent, for rapid iteration.
