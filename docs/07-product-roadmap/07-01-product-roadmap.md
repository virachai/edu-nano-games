## Product Roadmap & Phased Execution

### Phase 1: Foundation (v1.0.0)

- Project scaffold, shared game shell, and documentation structure (current state).

### Phase 2: Core Games (v1.1.0 – v1.3.0)

- **Game 1:** Vocabulary Matching Game (early–upper elementary)
- **Game 2:** Math Guardian Spaceship (upper elementary–middle school)
- **Game 3:** Chemistry Quiz Adventure (middle school–high school)

### Phase 3: Polish & Scale (v1.4.0+)

- Teacher dashboards and progress tracking
- Leaderboards and score persistence
- Additional subjects and grade bands
- Optional 3D titles powered by Three.js
